Game: Five Nights at Freddy's #1.
Goal: Surviving 5 Nights at a pizzeria with scary robots trying to kill you.
Player Action: You can turn on the lights, use the camera, use the doors, but it uses up your power, and once all your powers are gone, then you lose the game.
Feedback: It tells you the consequence if you win or lose, if you win then you move onto the next level, If you lose you have to replay the level again.
